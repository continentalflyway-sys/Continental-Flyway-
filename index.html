About Us

Connecting Bangladesh With The World

Continental Flyway is a Bangladesh-based travel and global mobility service provider dedicated to connecting people with international travel, career and business opportunities.

  • Visa processing assistance
  • Overseas employment & recruitment support
  • Air ticketing and hotel booking assistance
  • Tour packages, student visa & B2B facilities

Professional. Organized. Customer-focused.
