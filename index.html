About Us

Continental Flyway

Continental Flyway is a Bangladesh-based travel and global mobility service provider dedicated to connecting people with international travel, career and business opportunities.


We provide assistance in visa processing, overseas employment, recruitment, air ticketing, hotel booking, tour packages, student visa processing and B2B facilities.


Our goal is to make international travel and overseas opportunities more organized, convenient and accessible.

Our Mission

To provide professional, organized and customer-focused travel and global mobility services while connecting people with opportunities beyond borders.

Our Vision

To build Continental Flyway as a trusted international platform connecting Bangladesh with global travel, education, employment and business opportunities.
