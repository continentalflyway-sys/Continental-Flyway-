About Continental Flyway

About Us

CONTINENTAL FLYWAY is a travel and overseas opportunity-focused company based in Lalmatia, Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Our vision is simple — “THE WHOLE WORLD IS YOUR OFFICE.”

We provide a range of travel and international service solutions, including visa processing, air ticketing, hotel booking, tour packages and B2B facilities. We also assist clients with selected overseas employment and work visa processing opportunities according to available destinations, employer requirements and applicable regulations.

Our team aims to provide clear information, professional communication and practical support throughout the service process.

Whether you are planning an international journey, looking for a suitable tour package, or exploring overseas career opportunities, Continental Flyway is committed to making the process easier and more organized.

We believe in responsible service, transparent communication and building long-term relationships with our clients and business partners.
