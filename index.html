About Continental Flyway

Connecting People With Global Possibilities

CONTINENTAL FLYWAY is a travel and overseas opportunity-focused company based in Lalmatia, Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Our vision is simple — “THE WHOLE WORLD IS YOUR OFFICE.”

We provide visa processing, air ticketing, hotel booking, tour packages and B2B facilities. We also assist clients with selected overseas employment and work visa processing opportunities according to available destinations, employer requirements and applicable regulations.

Our goal is to provide clear information, professional communication and practical support throughout the service process.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide overseas job opportunities? +
We assist with selected overseas employment opportunities based on available employer vacancies, candidate qualifications and applicable immigration requirements. No job or visa approval is guaranteed.
Do you process work visas? +
We provide assistance with selected work visa and employment processing procedures. Final decisions are made by the relevant authorities and depend on eligibility and applicable laws.
Do you provide tourist visa assistance? +
Yes. We provide tourist and business visa processing assistance for selected destinations, subject to the relevant embassy, consulate and immigration requirements.
